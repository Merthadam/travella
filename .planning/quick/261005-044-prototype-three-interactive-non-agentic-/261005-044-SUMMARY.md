- B: split travel studio with a live traveler profile, richer desktop context.
- C: boarding-pass form and four-stop itinerary, strongest travel metaphor.

Preview command: `npm --prefix frontend run prototype:onboarding`.
URL: `http://127.0.0.1:5175/?preview=onboarding&variant=A` (B/C supported).

# Plan 03: My plans and lifecycle UI

## Objective

Replace the signed-in placeholder with the accessible My plans and Recently deleted surfaces described by the UI contract, including explicit lifecycle actions, exact title conflict recovery, safe stale-response handling, and immediate restore/open navigation.

## Artifacts this phase produces

- `frontend/src/PlansApp.jsx`: private lifecycle shell, active/deleted lists, dialogs, resume routing, and focus management.
- `frontend/src/plansApi.js`: allow-listed lifecycle calls with request IDs, revisions, and safe error parsing.
- `frontend/src/PlansApp.test.jsx`: lifecycle interaction and state/error coverage.

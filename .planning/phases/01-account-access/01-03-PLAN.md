## Must-haves

### Truths

- Password reset invalidates active sessions and requires a fresh sign-in with configured MFA still enforced.
- Deliberate sign-out clears the current browser session and later sign-in lands at My plans.
- Involuntary reauthentication retains only a safe internal destination and re-authorizes it for the new traveler.
- Release checks detect token, credential, recovery-code, reset-link, and private-data leakage.

### Artifacts this phase produces

- `src/auth/session-manager.ts`
- `src/auth/recovery-session.ts`
- refresh/signout routes, security suite, and account-access runbook

## Threat model

- Session fixation and stale refresh are mitigated by reset invalidation and the explicit 30-day policy.
- Open redirects are blocked by internal route sanitization and post-login authorization.
- Sensitive observability leakage is blocked by redacted event contracts and negative tests.

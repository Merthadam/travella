# Sketch 002: Dynamic Researcher

## Design Question
Which interaction structure makes a tool-using, source-grounded researcher feel capable and conversational while keeping the Plan and traveler in control?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/002-dynamic-researcher/index.html` or visit the local preview at `http://127.0.0.1:4175/.planning/sketches/002-dynamic-researcher/index.html?variant=A`. Choose A, B, or C from the top tabs or use the bottom-center arrows. The page also reads `?variant=A`, `?variant=B`, or `?variant=C`.

## Variants
- **A: Inline Research** — familiar conversation with readable tool activity and citations attached to the relevant answer.
- **B: Evidence Workspace** — conversation alongside a source and activity rail for inspecting what informed the answer.
- **C: Research Mission** — a central research run canvas with clear progress and evidence, plus a compact conversational command thread.

All tool calls, results, citations, map lookup, and save actions are simulated. No provider, database, or agent call is made.

## What to Look For
Compare how clearly each structure shows the researcher acting on a request, how well a follow-up stays connected to the original surf brief, and whether evidence is easy to inspect without turning the experience into a control dashboard.
